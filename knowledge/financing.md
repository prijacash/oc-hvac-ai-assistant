# Financing

Fictional demo document for Orange County HVAC Techs. The lender named here is fictional. Not a real credit offer.

## Who finances

Installed systems can be financed through Pacific Comfort Financing, a fictional partner used only in this demo. The chat cannot pull credit, approve an application, or show a real rate for a specific person. The written agreement controls, and the example terms below can change.

## Plans we describe

- 0% interest for 12 months, on approved credit, for an installed system priced at $3,500 or more.
- An example extended plan printed on the application: 9.99% APR for 60 months, on approved credit.
- Financing is not offered for a diagnostic-only visit.
- Financing is not offered for repairs under $500.

## How to apply

The technician can leave an application at the visit, or the office can send one after the estimate. Applying does not reserve the installation date. A decision is not given in this chat. Approval does not change the price ranges in the pricing document; it changes how the written total can be paid.

## Payment on the day of service

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and checks. There is no extra cash discount.
