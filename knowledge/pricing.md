# Pricing ranges

Fictional demo document for Orange County HVAC Techs. Every figure below is a made-up range for the demo. A written estimate after a visit controls. The assistant must not invent a price that is not printed here, and must not collapse a range into a single "exact" quote.

## Diagnostic visit

- Weekdays during posted business hours: $89.
- Saturday during posted hours: $129.
- After-hours emergency trip, including Sunday and holidays: $149, plus the repair.
- The weekday or Saturday diagnostic fee is waived when the customer approves a repair on that same visit.
- Comfort Club members never pay a diagnostic fee.
- The after-hours $149 trip charge is not waived.

## Common repairs

These ranges are the repair after diagnosis. They are not a promise that a particular home will land at the low end.

- Common air-conditioning repairs (capacitor, contactor, condenser fan motor, condensate problems, and similar): $180–$850.
- Compressor or evaporator coil replacement that is not a full system changeout: $1,800–$3,400.
- Refrigerant: we do not sell a top-off by the pound as a standalone fix. If the system is low, we look for the leak. When the leak is repairable, leak search and recharge typically runs $350–$750 depending on the refrigerant. We do not quote a price per pound in chat.
- Common furnace repairs (igniter, flame sensor, inducer motor, control board, and similar): $200–$900.
- Standard thermostat replacement, including a basic programmable thermostat: $180–$320.
- Labor to install a smart thermostat the customer already bought: $120.

## System replacements

Prices assume a typical single-family home and existing ductwork unless noted. Permits, crane access, and unusual electrical work change the written quote.

- Furnace replacement: $4,200–$8,900.
- Air conditioner replacement (outdoor unit and indoor coil, existing furnace stays): $6,800–$13,500.
- Heat pump changeout, existing ducts, typical 3–4 ton home: $9,500–$16,500.
- Electrical panel upgrade when the new equipment needs it: $1,800–$3,500 extra.
- Full duct replacement is quoted on site. We do not publish a duct-replacement price in chat.
- City permit fees on a replacement are added at cost. We do not quote permit fees in chat because they vary by city.

## Duct cleaning prices

- Single-story home up to about 1,800 square feet: $449–$649.
- Larger homes and two-story homes: $649–$899.

## Discounts

- Seniors age 65 and older, and active or retired military, receive 10% off repair labor with ID. The discount is not applied to equipment and not applied to installs.
- The senior or military labor discount does not stack with the Comfort Club 15% repair discount. The larger of the two applies.
- There is no unpublished cash discount.

## What we will not quote in chat

We will not give a single exact price for a home we have not seen. We will not quote a per-pound refrigerant price, a utility rebate dollar amount, a permit fee, or a duct-replacement total. Those are handled on the written estimate or at the visit, as this document says.
