# Services

Fictional demo document for Orange County HVAC Techs. Not a real business.

## Air conditioning repair

We repair central air conditioners and heat pumps that cool a home: weak airflow, short cycling, warm air from the vents, frozen lines, failed capacitors, contactors, fan motors, condensate leaks, and thermostats. A technician diagnoses the system before recommending a repair. Common repair ranges are in the pricing document. We service Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Bryant, American Standard, Mitsubishi, Daikin, and most other residential brands.

## Furnace repair

We repair gas and electric forced-air furnaces: no heat, short cycling, noisy inducers, failed igniters, flame sensors, and control boards. If we find a cracked heat exchanger, we shut the furnace down. We do not patch a cracked heat exchanger. Replacement is quoted on site using the furnace replacement range in the pricing document.

## Heat pumps

Heat pumps fit Orange County's mild climate because one system can heat and cool. We repair heat pumps and replace older air conditioners with heat pumps when the ducts and electrical service can support it. A panel upgrade, when the existing panel cannot carry the new equipment, is a separate price in the pricing document. We help customers check current utility and manufacturer rebates at the visit. We do not publish a rebate dollar amount.

## Comfort Club maintenance plan

Comfort Club is our fictional maintenance membership.

- One system: $189 per year.
- Two systems: $279 per year.
- Homes with more than two systems are quoted at the visit. There is no third published price.
- Two tune-ups each year: a spring cooling visit and a fall heating visit.
- The diagnostic fee is waived for members.
- Members receive 15% off repairs.
- Members get priority scheduling ahead of non-member routine calls.
- The plan does not cover parts, refrigerant, or a system replacement. Those are billed separately, with the member repair discount when the work is a repair.

## Duct cleaning

We clean supply and return ducts on a typical single-family home. A visual inspection of accessible ducts is included. Duct cleaning is housekeeping for dust in the ductwork. It is not mold remediation, not an asbestos project, and not a fix for a broken blower. Price ranges are in the pricing document. We do not offer dryer-vent-only cleaning.

## What we do not do

- We do not install solar panels, solar batteries, or pool equipment, including pool heaters.
- We do not service water heaters or perform plumbing.
- We do not service commercial rooftop units over 10 tons.
- We do not open refrigerant circuits, gas valves, or electrical panels as phone advice. Safe checks a homeowner can try are listed in the FAQ.
