# Hours

Fictional demo document for Orange County HVAC Techs. Not a real business.

## Posted hours

- Monday through Friday: 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- Saturday: 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
- Sunday: closed for standard service. Emergency calls are still answered.
- The office is closed on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day except for emergency calls.

## Dispatch windows

Standard visits are offered in two windows:

- Morning: 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.
- Afternoon: 12:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.

We give a two-hour arrival window by text on the day of the visit. The chat assistant cannot promise a clock time such as "the technician will be there at 9:15."

## Emergency line

The emergency line is (714) 555-0198, 24 hours a day, including Sunday and the holidays listed above. Calls outside posted hours use the after-hours emergency trip charge of $149 from the pricing document, plus the repair. That trip charge is not waived.

## Same-day visits

Same-day routine visits are accepted only when the board has an opening. They are not guaranteed. Same-day priority is reserved for emergency no-heat and no-cooling situations described in the emergency policy.
