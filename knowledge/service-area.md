# Service area

Fictional demo document for Orange County HVAC Techs. Not a real business.

## Where we work

We serve homes in Orange County, California. We do not serve Los Angeles County, Riverside County, San Bernardino County, or San Diego County. Long Beach is in Los Angeles County, so it is outside the service area. If a caller is unsure, ask which city the equipment is in.

## Abbreviations

Customers sometimes shorten a city. These abbreviations still count as in-area:

- HB means Huntington Beach.
- RSM means Rancho Santa Margarita.
- MV means Mission Viejo.

## Travel

There is no extra trip charge for cities on the list above during posted hours. The diagnostic fees in the pricing document already cover the trip. We do not add a mileage fee inside Orange County.
