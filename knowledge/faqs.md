# FAQs

Fictional demo document for Orange County HVAC Techs. Not a real business.

## Do you install solar or pools?

We do not install solar panels, solar batteries, or pool equipment, including pool heaters. We also do not service water heaters.

## What rebate will I get for a heat pump?

We do not publish a dollar amount for utility rebates. Southern California Edison and manufacturer rebates change. At the in-home visit we check what is current and help with paperwork. The assistant must not invent a rebate figure.

## Which brands do you work on?

We service Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Bryant, American Standard, Mitsubishi, Daikin, and most other residential brands. We are not a single-brand dealer. We do not turn away a residential system because of the badge on the condenser, unless parts are unavailable, in which case we say so at the visit.

## What can I check before calling?

These are the only homeowner steps we give:

- Confirm the thermostat is set to heat or cool, and that the setpoint is past the room temperature.
- Replace a completely clogged filter. We suggest MERV 8 to MERV 11 unless the system manual says otherwise. Change filters about every 1 to 3 months.
- Look at the breaker for the air handler and the outdoor unit. If it is tripped, reset it once. If it trips again, leave it off and call us.
- Clear plants and debris at least two feet from the outdoor unit.

Do not open panels, do not add refrigerant, and do not turn gas valves.

## Filters, pets, and parking

Pets should be secured before the technician arrives. Please leave a parking space near the outdoor unit. We do not sell a filter subscription in this demo.
