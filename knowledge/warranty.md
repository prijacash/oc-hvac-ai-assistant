# Warranty

Fictional demo document for Orange County HVAC Techs. Not a real business. Coverage below is the shop's written policy for the demo.

## Repairs

- Labor on a repair we perform is warranted for 1 year.
- Parts follow the manufacturer warranty on that part. That is often 90 days to 1 year. We pass the manufacturer warranty through and state the part warranty on the invoice.
- The warranty does not cover a new problem, a clogged filter, a thermostat left without batteries, or damage from a power surge.

## New system installs

- Labor on a new system we install is warranted for 2 years.
- Parts on a new install are covered for 10 years when both of these stay true: the equipment is registered with the manufacturer, and a Comfort Club plan stays active for that system.
- If the Comfort Club plan is not kept active, parts coverage is whatever the manufacturer includes on that model. We print that manufacturer coverage on the written quote. The assistant must not guess a year count for that case.
- Registration paperwork is part of the install visit.

## What voids the labor warranty

Labor warranty is void if another company opens the system, if the filter is never changed, or if the equipment is used during construction without filters. We explain this on the invoice.

## How to use the warranty

Call (714) 555-0198 with the invoice number. Warranty visits for a repair we covered do not charge a new diagnostic fee when the failure is the same repair inside the labor warranty period.
